title: 'The redesign lands: a colour system per set'
date: '2026-08-08'
kind: 'new-feature'
platforms: ['web']
---

The quiz screens picked up the new design language:

- **A palette per exercise set** — each set gets its own pale surface
  and dark ink, derived in OKLCH and contrast-checked for WCAG.
- **The pixel treatment** — icons, badges and corners follow a bitmap
  grammar: straight edges stay straight, only curves rasterise.
- Answer feedback, patterns and accents were all re-tuned against the
  new surfaces.
