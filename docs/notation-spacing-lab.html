Notation spacing — negation vs conjunction

Malik, 2026-08-25: “the dot seems too close to the S and might not register immediately.” It does, and the cause is measurable rather than a matter of taste.

The inversion. LaTeX classes \sim as a relation (like = or <), so KaTeX gives it thickspace, 5/18 em on both sides. \cdot is a binary operator, which gets medspace, 4/18 em.

So negation — which binds tightest — is drawn with more air than conjunction, which binds loosest. The spacing argues against the grouping, and the eye has to overrule it. Worse, two adjacent relations merge into one atom: ⊃∼ renders glued together, with the gap falling between the negation and the letter it negates.
