FQ1 Rec: white (direction 2026-08-15)

Ground — white, per the coloured footer

Malik’s direction: the footer takes the colour, so the FAQ (and the content region, per the marketing lab’s decided white) stays white — the footer becomes the page’s one colour moment, arriving with full force. The scheme grounds stay one dial away for honest comparison, and the simulated footer below shows the actual hand-off either way. What remains of the old question is FQ9: how much the white section may anticipate the footer’s colour.

FQ2 Rec: open-fill

Item treatment

Hairlines are today’s shape and the quietest; cards spend the nav’s D12 corner grammar but make five closed boxes compete with the footer below; open-fill keeps the closed list quiet and lets the one OPEN answer become a figure — the same clip-always-on/fill-on-paint move the nav hovers made, so it reads as the system, not a new device. Recommended because it puts the emphasis where the reading is.

FQ3 Judge

Does an answer get a surface?

Under hairlines or cards the answer can sit bare (today) or on a small sprite-cornered plate (R=12 — the hint-block register). The plate buys scannability when several items are open at once (type='multiple' ships) at the cost of a second surface per item. Under open-fill the question dissolves — the item IS the surface.

FQ4 Rec: eyebrow + display

Heading voice

Today’s centered 30 px bold vs the established voice: mono QUESTIONS eyebrow over the display stretch at up to 56 px. The section is the last one still speaking the old voice — after the nav, the marketing pages and the footer banner, the display treatment here is consistency, not novelty. Scale corrected 2026-08-15: the first cut ran the display to 56 px and Malik called it HUGE — it now caps at 42 px, the section register: an h2 inside a page, one clear step below any hero display, still ~2.7× the answer text.

FQ5 Judge — lean split

Layout

Centered is today’s shape. The split rail (heading + intro + “still curious” left, questions right) is the homepage’s first asymmetric section and keeps the heading in view while reading; it also gives the mailto a natural home. Two columns halve the scroll but pair badly with hairlines (two ragged rules) and open answers shift only their own column — judge it with cards.

Today’s four entries answer what/price/difference/LogiSkor. The two proposed additions carry the mission and classroom story — the donate case made in words, WITHOUT duplicating the header’s gem CTA (one oxygen mask per page level, the footer’s F6 rule). FQ7 Rec: edited

The copy pass

The verbatim copy carries a stale age claim (“It only has a couple of months” — written in 2023), a comma splice in the first answer, and “Logiskor”/“LogiSkor” disagreeing between title and body. The edit fixes those, tightens each answer toward its first sentence, and keeps Malik’s first-person voice. Either way the port should get real apostrophes (’ not ') — the strings in page.tsx are typewriter-quoted today.

FQ8 Rec: ship it

FAQPage JSON-LD

Valid schema.org/FAQPage markup on the homepage — snippet in the port notes. Honest expectations: Google restricted FAQ rich results to authoritative gov/health sites in 2023, so no SERP accordion — but the markup is exactly what AI answer engines read, and for “LogiCola” queries these six answers are the canonical source. Cheap, harmless, one <script> tag.

FQ9 Judge — the new question

Echo — does the white FAQ anticipate the footer?

With the colour concentrated in the footer, the white FAQ can meet it cold (Echo off — maximum contrast at the seam, the footer as a reveal) or send it ahead: the display heading in the footer’s ink (plum before the mint arrives — coloured ink on display only, the role split), the figure surfaces in the footer’s ground (the open plate turns mint — the page’s colour story told twice), or both. FQ10 Judge — Malik’s 2026-08-15 note

Contact — a name, and a calendar?

“Email me” vs the signed line (“I’m Malik, and I read every message”): the answers already speak in first person, and the support entry says the project is run by one person — naming that person makes the story concrete, and a named sender promises a reply in a way an anonymous “me” doesn’t. Provider undecided (Cal.com / Calendly); the link is inert here and the port should tag the booking click as its own PostHog event (book_call_click, source: 'faq_rail') so the hypothesis is measurable.

The instructor CTA now defaults to its OWN hairline-topped section under the list (FOR INSTRUCTORS eyebrow), which also gives the non-split layouts a home for it; the rail-inline draft and a rail-block variant stay on the dial for comparison.

FQ11 Judge — Malik’s catch

The head pair — an eyebrow that repeats its heading

QUESTIONS over FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS said the same thing twice — the eyebrow has to do work the heading doesn’t, or go. Three resolutions on the dial: no eyebrow (the heading is its own label — the default; the mono voice survives in the FOR INSTRUCTORS eyebrow and the index numbers), SUPPORT (an adjacent register — names the job, not the format), or the roles flipped: FAQ as the small label, “ASK AWAY” as the display voice. The flip is the only pair where both layers carry information; whether its tone fits the site is the judgement.

2 · Accessibility + port notes
  • Every text/ground pairing is walked through the ensure-contrast loop at render time and printed in the audit strip — muted tiers and the numbered index are derived, never eyeballed. The index starts from the scheme’s accent and darkens toward the type colour until it clears 4.5:1.
  • Focus finding: the site’s green #05A24B focus outline measures 2.85:1 on Set L’s mint — under 1.4.11’s 3:1 floor. The lab derives a per-scheme focus colour (green mixed toward the type colour until ≥3:1) and the audit strip marks when the derived value is in play. The port should hoist this into the token file that D11’s switch will edit.
  • Structure is unchanged from the app: page h2, trigger inside an h3 (Radix Header), button with aria-expanded/aria-controls, panel as role="region". type='multiple' is kept. Triggers exceed the 44 px floor.
  • Motion: open/close is a 240 ms quart-out height reveal, chevron rotates in 200 ms; both behind prefers-reduced-motion here. Port check: the app’s animate-accordion-up/down keyframes should sit behind the same guard.
  • Port shape: the visual change is classNames on the existing shadcn wrapper plus (for open-fill) an always-on clip-path from spriteClip(0, 16) — generators from lib/pixel.ts, never a re-implementation. Card shadows go on a wrapper as filter: drop-shadow (the Viewport rule). The rail’s mailto needs a real address decided; it is inert here.
  • FAQPage JSON-LD, mirroring whatever copy ships (Google requires the markup to match the visible text):

{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "FAQPage",

      "@type": "Question",

    }
    // … one entry per visible question
  ]
}
</script>
