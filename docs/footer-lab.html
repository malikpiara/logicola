The mobile reflow is designed, not accidental: subscribe-first stacks banner → brand (can + mission) → link groups as a two-up grid (Resources · Follow, Legal wrapping under) → bottom bar → band; columns and compact collapse to the same stack with the subscribe in document order. The pill goes full width; the social chips keep their 30 px visual but gain 44 px tap boxes (HIG floor — also applied on desktop); the band renders at 390 px so the camo features stay in proportion. Safe-area bottom padding is the port’s job, same as the sheets.

What the dials cover. Scheme: the D11 candidates plus today’s white as baseline (Brand · mint stays dead). Layout: the current three-column structure, a subscribe-first banner (the marketing newsletter section arriving at the footer), and a one-row compact. Subscribe: inline row vs the inverted card vs none. Pattern: none, the camo band (fine or giant — the § 4p survivor), or the full field with the content in a clearing (“the clearing is the panel”, applied to the footer); band position tries top (a separator from the page) and bottom (the news-band precedent). Mark: today’s text wordmark, the can, or the lockup. Patterns spend inks and accents only — the brand lab’s forced inversion.

1 · Decisions to take
F1 Leaning Set L (2026-08-14)

Footer ground

The footer is the first surface that could commit to a scheme ground site-wide (the header stays white for now by Malik’s call). Malik’s favourite state wears Set L — now the lab default — which also matches the D11 lean recorded since 2026-08-13. Not yet the ship decision; colour is revisited after the nav round.

F2 Malik: subscribe-first

Structure

The lab’s initial lean was columns; Malik’s favourite is the subscribe-first banner (2026-08-14) — the newsletter as the footer’s opening move, with the mission line kept under the can. Recorded as the default; the columns and compact structures stay one dial away.

F3 Decided form, placed by dial

Subscribe: the release-notes pill

The form itself is no longer a question — it’s the marketing lab’s locked pick, verbatim: the white sprite-clipped pill with the Subscribe gem inside it (form: 'pill' + btnColors, with an ensure-guard for green-ink schemes the original never met). What this lab still decides is WHERE it sits: row in the column, the inverted card, or the subscribe-first banner.

F4 The personality question

Pattern at the bottom

Malik’s instinct: “a pattern applied to the bottom might give some personality.” The camo band is the shape that survived every prior judgement (nav § 4p; the marketing pages’ news-band). The full field-with-clearing is the maximal version. Bottom placement closes the page the way the news-band closes the newsletter; top placement reads as a page/footer seam instead.

F5 Mark or wordmark

The footer identity

Today’s green text wordmark is a logotype (exempt from the contrast floors). The can mark recolours by scheme (body ink, knockout ground — the marketing nav’s grammar). The lockup shows both; brand-decisions’ avatar rule (“logo alone”) was social-specific and doesn’t bind here.

F7 Rec: two-up groups

Mobile stacking

At 390 px the favourite reads banner → brand → groups → bar → band. Link groups go two-up rather than a single stack — eleven links in one column is a scroll of grey; two columns halve it and still hit 44 px row targets. The single-stack alternative is one CSS line if the two-up feels tight on real hardware.

F6 Rec: keep + gem Donate later

Content inventory

Everything in today’s footer survives: Resources (Keyboard keeps its 20 px NEW gem), Follow, Legal, the Some-Rights copyright, the pixel social icons (represented as mono links here; the shipped icons port as-is). Donate is deliberately NOT duplicated into the footer while the header carries the gem CTA — one oxygen mask per page level.

2 · Accessibility + port notes
  • Every text/ground pairing below is walked through the ensure-contrast loop at render time and printed in the audit strip — muted tiers are derived, never eyeballed. The wordmark is the one logotype exemption, flagged when it dips under 3:1.
  • The email field keeps a real (visually hidden) label — a placeholder is not a label (WCAG 3.3.2); type="email" + autocomplete="email" in the port for the right keyboard and autofill.
  • The subscribe button is the sprite-clipped gem: type-colour ground, scheme-ground text — the same CTA rule as the header’s Donate. 44 px tall (HIG floor).
  • Pattern layers are aria-hidden decoration; the band never sits behind text, and the field mode carves a clearing under the content (measured per render, the marketing hero’s construction).
  • Port: the form posts to the Supabase capture stub (provider undecided — docs/blog-release-notes-stack memory); generators come from lib/patterns.ts, not a re-implementation; the mark mirrors public/logicola-wordmark.svg.
